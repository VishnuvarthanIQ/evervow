Common Questions

Pricing & Booking FAQs

Are there any hidden fees or surprise charges? +
Never. The package price you see covers all consultations, script writing, revisions, officiating, and marriage license filing. The only additional costs are optional add-ons you explicitly select or travel beyond 50 miles round-trip from our studio.
Can we start with The Standard package and add a rehearsal or vow coaching later? +
Yes, absolutely! Many couples book The Standard Bespoke package initially and decide to add vow writing coaching or rehearsal coordination 2 to 3 months before the wedding. You can also upgrade to The Premium package at any time.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept all major credit and debit cards, ACH bank transfers, Apple Pay, and Zelle through our secure online client portal. Need a 3-part monthly payment plan instead of 50/50? Just let us know—we're happy to accommodate at no extra cost.
What is your postponement or date-change policy? +
Life happens! If you need to postpone or move your wedding date, your retainer transfers in full to your new date at no additional fee (subject to officiant availability, or covered by one of our wonderful associate officiants).
How are destination weddings and travel fees calculated? +
The first 50 miles round-trip from our studio (or 75 miles with The Premium package) are included at no charge. Beyond that, standard mileage is billed at $0.85/mile. For destination weddings requiring airfare or overnight lodging, we provide a flat, all-inclusive travel quote upfront before you book.
