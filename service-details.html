The Bespoke Ceremony

Our signature offering: a fully customized, deeply personal script written entirely from scratch.

Overview

The Bespoke Ceremony is designed for couples who want their wedding to feel like an authentic reflection of their relationship. We don't use templates. We don't copy and paste from the internet. Every word is carefully chosen to tell your specific story.

This package involves a deep dive into your history, your values, and your vision for the future, resulting in a ceremony that will have your guests laughing, crying, and saying, "That was so them."

What's Included

In-Depth Strategy Session

A 60-minute deep dive via Zoom to outline the vibe, tone, and rituals.

Custom Story Questionnaire

Detailed separate questionnaires to capture both of your unique perspectives.

Unlimited Revisions

You have complete editorial control. We tweak it until it's flawless.

Vow Writing Assistance

Review, editing, and formatting of your personal vows (printed on keepsake cards).

Vendor Coordination

We work directly with your planner, photographer, and DJ for seamless cues.

Legal Filing

Proper execution and prompt mailing of your marriage license.

The Timeline

6-9 Months Out

Initial consultation, contract signing, and retainer paid to secure your date.

3-4 Months Out

Questionnaires are sent out. We schedule our deep-dive strategy session.

1-2 Months Out

First draft of the ceremony is delivered. Revisions begin. Personal vows are reviewed.

2 Weeks Out

Final script approval. Coordination emails sent to vendor team.

Wedding Day

I arrive early to check in with vendors, deliver your vows, and rock your ceremony.
