3 Vow Prompts to Spark Your Draft Tonight

Pour a cup of tea, set a 10-minute timer, and answer one of these questions without editing yourself.

Prompt 01 / Origin

The Quiet Realization

"Skip the grand milestones for a moment—what was the small, ordinary Tuesday afternoon or road-trip detour when you looked at your partner and realized, 'I am home'?"

Great for your opening paragraph
Prompt 02 / Gratitude

The Unseen Anchor

"What is one quality your partner possesses that has made you a calmer, braver, or kinder version of yourself over the years?"

Ideal for the heart of your vows
Prompt 03 / Promise

The Specific Tomorrow

"Pair one profound lifelong vow (standing together in storms) with one lighthearted daily promise only the two of you share."

Perfect for a memorable finale
