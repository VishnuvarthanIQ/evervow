The Processional

Music Cue: Canon in D
[Music begins as the seating of the grandmothers and mothers commences. Groom James and Best Man David take their places at the altar alongside Officiant Sarah Jenkins. Groomsmen and Bridesmaids enter in pairs. The bridal entrance fanfare plays. Bride Emma enters escorted by her father.]
Speaker: Instrumental Music & Action Duration: 4 mins
2

Welcome & Opening Remarks

Speaker: Rev. Sarah
"Welcome, family and friends! We are gathered here today on this breathtaking afternoon under the vines of St. Clair Gardens to celebrate one of life's greatest blessings: the union of Emma and James. You have journeyed from near and far, bringing your warmth, love, and support. As they stand before you today, they invite you not just as spectators, but as the witnesses and guardians of their promises."
Speaker: Officiant Duration: 3 mins
3

Guest Reading: "Union"

Reader: Sister Chloe
"You have known each other from the first glance of acquaintance to this point of commitment. At some point, you decided to marry. From that moment until this, much has passed through your thoughts... Now, you shall say a few words that take you across a threshold of life, and things will never quite be the same."
Author: Robert Fulghum Duration: 3 mins
4

The Couple's Love Story

Speaker: Rev. Sarah
"Seven years ago, on a rainy Tuesday coffee run in San Francisco, an accidental umbrella mix-up sparked the very first conversation between Emma and James. What began over spilled lattes blossomed into weekend hikes across Muir Woods, shared cooking experiments, and an unwavering devotion that weathered every storm..."
Custom Love Story Narrative Duration: 5 mins
5

Exchange of Personal Vows

Emma & James
"James, from the moment you entered my life, you brought a calm and steady warmth that felt like home. I promise to stand by your side through every season, to laugh with you until our sides ache, to encourage your wildest ambitions, and to never let a day pass without showing you how deeply you are loved."
"Emma, you are my best friend, my compass, and my greatest dream come true. I promise to choose you every single morning, to hold your hand through the storms, to celebrate your victories as my own, and to love you unconditionally for all the days of our lives."
Target Length: 250 - 320 Words Each Duration: 4 mins
6

Ring Exchange & Unity Candle

Rings & Unity Flame
"These rings are circles with no beginning and no end, symbolizing love that knows no bounds. James, place this ring on Emma's finger and repeat: 'I give you this ring as a symbol of my unending love, my friendship, and my forever promise.' Emma, place this ring on James's finger..."
Best Man David presents rings Duration: 4 mins
7

Pronouncement & The First Kiss

The Grand Finale
"Now, having pledged your love and exchanged your sacred vows before these witnesses, by the power vested in me, I joyfully pronounce you husband and wife! James, you may kiss your beautiful bride! Family and friends, it is my distinct honor to present to you for the first time: Mr. and Mrs. James and Emma Miller!"
Music Cue: "Signed, Sealed, Delivered" Duration: 2 mins
